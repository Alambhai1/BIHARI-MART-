Seller Commission & Payment Policy

BIHARI MART Platform Commission: 3%

BIHARI MART will charge only the Seller a 3% platform commission on successful sales. No platform commission will be charged to the Buyer.

Our Commitments

  • Commission will apply only after a successful sale, subject to the platform terms.
  • The applicable commission amount will be clearly shown to the Seller before order confirmation.
  • No hidden platform commission or undisclosed platform fee will be charged.
  • Applicable taxes and statutory deductions, if any, will be handled according to Indian law.
